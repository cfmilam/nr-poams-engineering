## 2. NUMEROLOGY TEST — Fitted, Not Forced

**Mainstream assessment and why.** Wyler (C. R. Acad. Sci. 269A, 743 (1969); 271A, 186 (1971)) drew brief attention (Physics Today, Aug. 1971; Robertson, PRL 27, 1545) and was then dismantled, decisively by **Gilmore (PRL 28, 462 (1972))**, on grounds that remain fatal:

- **Volumes of bounded domains are not invariant numbers.** They depend on the arbitrary normalization (the "radius") of the domain and on which measure you choose (Euclidean vs. Bergman/invariant vs. Šilov boundary measure). Rescale the domain and every "volume" rescales. The pure numbers π⁵/1920 and 8π⁴ are **convention artifacts**, and the conventions were selected after the target was known.
- **No dynamical bridge.** There is no derivation — none — of why a coupling constant should equal (volume ratio)×(fourth root of another volume) rather than its square, its logarithm, or any other combination. The ¼ power ("interior→boundary reduction") is not a mathematical operation that produces couplings; it is a phrase.
- **The look-elsewhere effect.** The space of expressions built from small integers, factorials, powers of π, and rational exponents is dense enough to hit any 5–6 digit target. Example: **4π³ + π² + π = 137.0363**, an entirely unrelated "geometric" expression that matches to the same precision as Wyler. Two independent formulas can't both be the true topological identity for α; therefore 6-digit agreement carries almost no evidential weight.
- **The structural killer:** measured low-energy α includes QED vacuum-polarization contributions from the *entire charged particle spectrum* (muons, taus, quarks/hadrons), and α **runs with energy scale** (α(m_Z) ≈ 1/128). A pure geometric constant cannot know the lepton and quark masses. Any geometry-only formula must specify *which* α it derives and *why at that scale* — Wyler doesn't, and answering would introduce exactly the free parameters the claim forbids.

**Verdict on this test:** curve-fitting. Matches ~6 digits then diverges; the components were tunable and were tuned.

## 3. POAMS INTERPRETATION — Narration, Not Derivation

Applying Discipline 1 honestly: the POAMS ontological claim — that α, being dimensionless, should be a *self-ratio* of angular-momentum structure rather than a contingent input — is coherent, is a legitimate metaphysical position, and is actually a respectable framing of a question mainstream physics also asks (Feynman's "magic number"). But the question here is narrower: **does POAMS independently force Wyler's specific factors?** Test each:

- **Why SO(4,2)?** POAMS's own structure ("three AM organization modes + time") most naturally motivates SO(3) or SO(3,1). SO(4,2) — the conformal group — enters only because *Wyler used it*. No POAMS text derives "5 complex dimensions" from AM ontology before knowing the answer.
- **Why the Cartan type IV domain, and why the Euclidean volume normalization?** POAMS is silent. These are precisely the tunable choices Gilmore identified.
- **Why the ¼ power?** "10 real dims → 4 real dims" would suggest 4/10 or 2/5, not 1/4, by any naive dimensional logic. The ¼ is what the target number requires.
- **Why 9?** "3×3 Lorentz-mode coupling channels" — but why do channels multiply into the coupling linearly? Why not 3, 6, or 9²? The number 9 is retro-fitted; the story is written after the arithmetic.

Decisive test for retrofit vs. derivation: **could a POAMS theorist, given only the AM ontology and no knowledge of 1/137, have written down this formula and no other?** Plainly no. Every branch point has POAMS-flavored narration available for multiple options; the one that lands on 137.036 was selected. That is post-hoc rationalization. POAMS does not derive Wyler's formula; it *decorates* it.
