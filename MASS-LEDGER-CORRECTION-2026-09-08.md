\[

\[
[x_{i\alpha},q_{i\alpha}]=i\hbar(1-1/n).
\]

\[

\frac{9}{8h}\frac{\hbar^2}{m\langle x_i^2\rangle}

Jacobi coordinate can place one factor in a reduced mass, but those Jacobi energies cannot then
