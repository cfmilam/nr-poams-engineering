Bench reference — reading extant instrument labels and unit measures inside the one-substance account. Operate every instrument by its manual; book every result in these accounts. Nothing on this card is evidence.

Three rules at the point of measurement
  1. Separate columns. Native records and extant labels never share a column. Adopt numbers, never stories.
  2. Stated tariffs. Every bridge between an extant measure and a native account is a measured, declared tariff — never silently derived, never imported by habit.
  3. A label is not an ontology. When an instrument's name tempts a mechanism — field, wave, carrier, force — quote it as the record's manner of speaking and post only the number.
