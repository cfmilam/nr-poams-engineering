# Electricity Complete-Apparatus Response

## Program question

Across a complete source–line–load–return apparatus, do source work, return work, recoverable state energy, dissipation, internal force and strain, and whole-apparatus force and torque reconcile under ordinary calibrated response? If a residual remains, does it depend reproducibly on link completion or load readiness under matched controls?

No favorable terminal is allowed to stand in for the apparatus. Electrical, stored-energy, dissipative, internal-mechanical, and whole-apparatus accounts remain distinct.

## Discrete conductor-map branch

The current theoretical candidate adds a stricter apparatus-map question to this program. A balanced two-rail preparation is represented as one simultaneous composite state. A physical rail exchange `X`, a balanced local successor `U0`, and an exchange-even terminal effect `E+` conditionally imply an exact null for a differential exchange-odd preparation. A small calibrated defect `epsilon` is predicted to open amplitude at first order and completed terminal weight or power at second order.

This is presently a **verified mathematical candidate, not a conductor result**. Its finite-graph conservation and symmetry behavior have been checked synthetically. No physical retained seat, action metric, local successor, terminal effect, opportunity rate, or event-energy tariff has yet been identified from apparatus records.

The experimental branch must therefore:

1. select a modular, physically swappable two-rail fixture;
2. acquire raw complex voltage/current, timing, thermal, mechanical, calorimetric, and external-channel records with declared units;
3. calibrate a positive action metric independently from reversible energy and timing records;
4. identify one local operational map on training configurations and freeze it;
5. predict held-out lengths, section orderings, cadences, and defect locations;
6. test the balanced null and the first-order-amplitude/second-order-power defect law;
7. close source, load, recoverable storage, heat, mechanical, external, and remainder accounts; and
8. only then estimate absolute opportunity rate, event tariff, and power.

Failure of one frozen local map to transfer across the held-out fixtures rejects the minimal homogeneous graph law. Choosing an action metric after the fact merely to make a measured transfer map unitary is not confirmation.
