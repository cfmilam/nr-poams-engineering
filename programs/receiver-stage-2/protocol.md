# Receiver Stage 2 conditional protocol

## 1. Scope and mandatory entry

The experiment tests whether preregistered sink states can be discriminated from the frozen source-local record after ordinary pathways are measured and vetoed. It begins only when the exact parent branches satisfy all of the following:

- Stage 0: M0 raw occurrence, independent M1 replay, registered H1 passage, physical repeat and held-out geometry;
- Stage 1: reproduced C2 passage, second sink/article, held-out geometry, closed B1/B2 ledgers, stable latency/settling, finite absent bound and no surviving artifact replica;
- unchanged source, primary observable, state signature, reference planes, bandwidth, clock/error model and control limits;
- immutable parent hashes and a signed unresolved-findings statement.

If a value changes, identify the affected parent qualifications and repeat them before Stage-2 acquisition. Historical claims and exhibit animations cannot substitute for entry data.

## 2. Hypotheses and prohibited inferences

Let the frozen symbol alphabet be `X={x0,...,xK-1}`, implemented only by Stage-1-qualified sink states. Let `R_A` be the source-local raw record and `Z` the complete ordinary-path monitor set.

- **T0 / no symbol relation:** held-out classification is equivalent to the preregistered null performance and `R_A` adds no predictive value beyond `Z`.
- **T1 / ordinary-path relation:** symbols are discriminated, but a measured conducted/radiated/optical/acoustic/mechanical/thermal/timing/software path predicts the score or reproduces it by injection.
- **T2 / candidate residual relation:** frozen decoder performance generalizes to held-out blocks, exceeds the disjoint acceptance bound after conditioning on `Z`, all controls pass and complete receipts close.
- **TX / contradiction:** wrong chronology, state confusion, nonclosing receipts, remount failure or behavior outside all registered families.

T2 is not proof of nonlocality, superluminal signalling, source-before-sink influence, carrierlessness, security, identity addressing or capacity. Do not use those words as conclusions.

## 3. Alphabet, schedule and source discipline

Use the smallest alphabet justified by Stage 1, initially binary: one qualified intended-ready state and one qualified control state. Do not choose alphabet states by inspecting Stage-2 separability. Freeze symbol duration, guard interval, transition constraint, sequence length, balance, run-length distribution and pilot/confirmatory split.

The source command remains fixed and unkeyed. Symbol information enters only through the sink-state schedule. A cryptographically committed sequence is generated offline, escrowed and loaded locally at the sink. The source-side acquisition has no access to labels, future schedule, sink controller, network time or operator cues. Include catch blocks with no state changes, sham transitions and delayed/replayed schedules.

## 4. Architecture A — local discriminator

Use the qualified Stage-1 source/sink pair in separate shielded enclosures inside one laboratory. Supply independent batteries or characterized isolated power; use one calibrated timing system only during metrology, then test the acquisition mode with all nonessential cross-boundary timing/control links removed. Boundaries:

- **B0:** source article, local supply/controller and native source sensors;
- **B1:** all intended guided/near-field routes and sink article to frozen reference planes;
- **B2:** complete reaction system, enclosures, mounts, supplies, grounds, clocks and environment;
- **B3:** all possible information crossings between symbol generator and source acquisition, including people and software.

Freeze an exhaustive B3 crossing ledger. An undocumented live crossing invalidates a block.

## 5. Architecture B — separated replication

After a frozen local T2 candidate, reproduce in independently powered rooms or sites with known coordinates and separation. Requirements:

- offline committed schedules delivered before isolation;
- no network, radio, cable, optical timing fibre or live command path between sites during blocks;
- independent acquisition clocks disciplined before and after, never silently steered during the run;
- common-event calibration before/after using a declared transported clock or reciprocal calibration method with uncertainty;
- continuous RF spectral monitoring over the justified range, plus calibrated out-of-band spot checks;
- optical line-of-sight closure and photodetectors at penetrations;
- microphones, accelerometers/seismometers and pressure sensors;
- supply, ground, neutral, shield, plumbing/HVAC and structural-path current/transfer monitoring;
- synchronized video/access logs and operator isolation sufficient to exclude cueing;
- randomized separation, orientation or enclosure transformations where feasible.

Separated clocks support ordering only within their calibrated uncertainty. Software receipt times and internet time are not primary evidence.

## 6. Required conditions

Each balanced confirmatory campaign contains:

1. intended symbol states;
2. detuned versions at the registered offsets;
3. matched dummy schedule;
4. frequency/gross-load-matched clone schedule;
5. sink-absent fixture with sham commands;
6. source/sink role reversal if both articles were qualified bidirectionally; otherwise label it a separate branch;
7. geometry/orientation reversal with ordinary loads matched;
8. schedule reversal and time-shifted replay;
9. multiple-sink challenge;
10. zero-information balanced transitions, catch blocks and duplicated-label controls;
11. direct ordinary-channel injections carrying the same symbol sequence.

Source/sink reversal is a physical control, not semantic relabeling. It may test reciprocity only when both directions have independently qualified reference planes, state witnesses and parent-stage performance.

## 7. Leakage and ordinary-channel qualification

Before confirmatory acquisition, transmit the registered symbol sequence deliberately through each credible path and fit its transfer into `R_A`:

- **conducted:** mains, protective earth, shields, grounds, signal/command cables, clock/fibre hardware, USB/network, plumbing and fixture conduction;
- **RF/EM:** near-field capacitive/inductive injection, radiated carriers, harmonics, intermodulation, rectification, enclosure apertures and monitor blind bands;
- **optical:** visible/IR emission, status LEDs, fibres, windows and detector saturation;
- **acoustic/mechanical:** airborne sound, table/floor vibration, cable strain, switching impulse, fans/HVAC and structural propagation;
- **thermal:** sink actuator heat, air/plumbing transport and sensor drift;
- **software/timing:** shared files, metadata, filenames, process scheduling, pseudorandom seeds, NTP/PTP/GPS/trigger coupling, packet residue, timestamp skew and analysis leakage;
- **human/analyst:** audible/visible cues, run-order knowledge, optional exclusions and decoder tuning.

Injections span or exceed the candidate score/amplitude and bandwidth while remaining linear or explicitly modeling nonlinearity. Freeze transfer functions and veto thresholds before unblinding. Monitor non-detection is a finite bound over stated coverage, not proof of absence.

## 8. Timing and latency custody

Record four distinct times: scheduled symbol boundary, sink command, independent physical state-witness crossing and source-record onset. Only hardware events are primary. Calibrate sensor delays, filters, cable delays, clock offset/drift/jitter and change-point bias by blind synthetic and physical injections.

The registered latency is the interval from physical sink-state witness to source-record onset with the full uncertainty budget. Do not subtract `distance/c`, align traces to maximize correlation, search arbitrary lags, or infer negative/instantaneous latency post hoc. Ordinary propagation envelopes are explicit comparators. Any later claim about timing versus separation requires at least three distances, independently calibrated clocks, a frozen onset estimator and disjoint uncertainty intervals; it is outside this protocol's channel decision.

## 9. Energy and angular-momentum receipts

At source and sink record voltage/current/IQ, supply energy, dissipation, storage, actuator work and temperature. At B2 record force/torque, fixture strain, vibration and every material crossing capable of carrying linear or angular momentum. Count one two-ended event once; do not add endpoint readings as two event energies.

For every symbol transition report:

- source and sink work integrals with calibration covariance;
- stored/recovered/dissipated energy and closure residual;
- actuator torque/angular impulse and support reaction;
- linear/angular momentum residual across B2;
- ordinary-channel energy upper bounds and monitor coverage.

A nonclosing ledger blocks attribution. An observed source classifier does not by itself identify the energy or angular-momentum route.

## 10. Calibration, pilot and freeze

Run an unblinded non-claim pilot containing every condition and artifact injection. Estimate block covariance, autocorrelation, state-transition failures, monitor detection limits, clock stability, decoder variance, day/remount effects and the smallest technically meaningful advantage. Use the pilot only to freeze hardware, alphabet, windows, feature map, decoder, hyperparameters, equivalence bands, maximum blocks and exclusions. Pilot trials never enter confirmatory inference.

The confirmatory analysis container receives only raw channels and opaque trial IDs. Training uses a prespecified subset; the held-out set is opened once. No feature, lag, bandwidth, symbol duration, decoder or condition is changed after held-out access.

## 11. Statistical decision sequence

The block or independently randomized sequence—not waveform samples—is the unit. Preserve temporal dependence. Report the full confusion matrix, balanced accuracy/error with finite-sample intervals, calibration curves and performance by day, remount, state, symbol, run length and architecture.

The decision sequence is:

1. verify state preparation and custody;
2. verify decoder above its frozen null on held-out local blocks;
3. demonstrate incremental predictive value of `R_A` beyond the complete monitor set `Z`;
4. fail to reproduce the effect through each registered direct injection;
5. pass dummy, detuned, clone, absent, reversal and schedule controls;
6. pass physical remount and second article;
7. reproduce with the separated architecture.

Only after all seven may a separate information analysis estimate a lower confidence bound on conditional mutual information for the tested input distribution. Do not infer Shannon capacity from one input distribution or decoder. Capacity requires a defined memory model, stationarity scope, input-cost constraint, channel-law estimation and an optimized achievable-rate/converse analysis. Until then use “symbol discrimination,” never “capacity,” “bandwidth” or “bits per second” as an earned physical result.

For planning a binary paired contrast with block-level standard deviation `s_d` and smallest meaningful effect `delta`,

`n_pairs ~= [(z_(1-alpha/2)+z_(1-beta)) s_d / delta]^2`,

inflated for design effect, multiplicity, state failures, remount/site heterogeneity and held-out allocation. Simulation of the actual frozen decoder/pipeline determines the maximum count.

## 12. Useful nulls and adverse results

- Null local discrimination bounds the registered alphabet/source coordinate in the tested apparatus.
- Local positive plus injection replica identifies an ordinary coupling and improves the apparatus model.
- Local positive plus separated null bounds the candidate to the local environmental/crossing class.
- Dummy/clone success retires claimed identity selectivity at the achieved sensitivity.
- Detuning flatness retires the registered readiness curve, not every possible coupling.
- Sink-absent decoding exposes leakage, parasitic sinks or analysis contamination.
- Reversal failure constrains reciprocity only for a bidirectionally qualified branch.
- Wrong chronology or nonclosing receipts is adverse evidence, not an invitation to move the window.
- A rigorous null does not refute POAMS ontology; it retires the tested engineering mapping and sensitivity.

## 13. Safety and stop rules

Inherit all Stage-0/1 interlocks. Perform RF exposure and leakage assessment; laser/optical classification; electrical stored-energy and touch-safety review; fire/thermal review; cryogen, vacuum, pressure and motion review if introduced; hearing/vibration limits; battery containment; emergency stop and lockout/tagout. Separation never removes the need for safe local supervision. Safety overrides blinding, and every intervention is retained.

Abort on interlock trip, clipping, clock unlock, state-witness failure, unmonitored crossing, enclosure breach, unsafe temperature/reflected power, unauthorized network activity or operator cue. Abort rules are frozen; an aborted trial is never silently discarded.

## 14. Promotion gate

A candidate experimental channel requires unchanged local and separated passage, independent replay, remount/second article, all ordinary-path vetoes, receipts, frozen held-out decoder and public release of raw custody sufficient for replication. Independent replication is required before engineering range or service claims. A later quantitative antenna/emission law remains a separate mathematical debt.
