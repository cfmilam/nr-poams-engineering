## 1. Existing work carried forward

The public EXP-S3 protocol is already a rigorous preregistration architecture. This package does not supersede it. It carries forward:

- one non-overlapping AM and energy boundary;
- co/anti × free/locked preparations;
- translation-null, orbit-onset, plane-tracking, orbit-off orientation, and locked-gimbal gates;
- odd torque/power/impulse/work estimands;
- endpoint/path separation;
- direct measurement of ordinary bearing, drive, support, electrical, magnetic, aerodynamic, thermal, pressure, acoustic, vibration, and trajectory paths;
- scaling and independent-replication gates;
- explicit bans on topology-to-torque, alpha, instantaneous, Venus/galaxy/atom, propulsion, and universal-coefficient leaps.

The YIG package contributes the four-account separation, B1/B2 discipline, independent retained-state witness, remount/second-article structure, pilot-derived sizing, and immutable custody pattern. The Einstein–de Haas/Barnett package contributes the measured fact that magnetic order and mechanical AM can exchange and the requirement to book coil/lattice/support reactions; it does not establish the proposed gyro support law.

## 4. Material gaps before execution

1. **Apparatus down-select:** rotor energy/speed, drive method, gimbal topology, platform trajectory, chamber, and boundary `B` are not selected.
2. **Safety engineering:** no rotor stress/critical-speed, containment, fragment, brake, failure-mode, service-life, or independent safety review exists for a chosen design.
3. **Pilot metrology:** no loaded support noise spectrum, Allan deviation, drift/covariance, structural-mode, or achieved calibration package.
4. **Inertia and state calibration:** no selected rotor/platform inertia tensors, remount repeatability, attitude accuracy, or `S/L` equivalence bands.
5. **Closure:** no physical inventory and measured AM/energy boundary closure for ordinary maneuvers.
6. **Artifact transfer functions:** no direct force/torque, tilt, vibration, thermal, bearing, magnetic, electrical, cable, acoustic, aerodynamic, or software injection records.
7. **Earth-orientation discrimination:** one site provides limited geometric leverage; long-duration drift may alias solar/thermal and sidereal terms.
8. **Topology witness:** no framed material curve or imaging/reduction architecture; twist/writhe language must remain operational rather than topological.
9. **Retained-state witness:** no independent post-drive coordinate; that branch is dormant.
10. **Statistics:** smallest effect, equivalence region, block count, multiplicity allocation, and maximum runtime require independent pilot data.
11. **Replication:** no second rotor/apparatus campaign exists.

## 5. Cost and schedule reality

The package is requirements-ready, not quote-ready. Dominant cost drivers are rated rotor/containment, gimbal/orbit platform fabrication, multi-axis metrology, vacuum compatibility, optical tracking, synchronized DAQ, and engineering safety review. Existing lab assets could change cost by an order of magnitude. Therefore a dollar BOM before inventory and pilot down-select would be decorative arithmetic.

Recommended phased commitment:

1. **Phase A — desktop/safety design:** boundary inventory, rotor dynamics, containment and FMEA; no spin.
2. **Phase B — low-energy metrology bench:** sensors, dummy, stationary steady/transient branches, injections.
3. **Phase C — integrated gimbal/orbit rig:** translation/orbit, plane tracking, free/locked matrix.
4. **Phase D — confirmatory blinded campaign:** frozen after pilot; no pilot reuse.
5. **Phase E — independent replication:** separate rotor and preferably separate apparatus/site.

## 6. Finite-null and adverse-result meaning

- A stationary steady-spin null bounds support coupling to achieved bulk spin in the tested axes/speeds/bandwidth; it does not test containing orbit.
- A transient null bounds the registered impulse/work law over achieved `dS/dt`, jerk, and controller paths.
- Translation-null plus orbit-null bounds the selected `S·L_containing` response over achieved radius/rate/plane; it does not prove no other geometry could couple.
- A free/locked ordinary closure retires anomaly language for that observed motion even if the video is dramatic.
- Failure of Earth-axis orientation with adequate sensitivity rejects that registered branch at the site/configuration; it cannot be renamed local-vertical after unblinding.
- Failure to instantiate topology means no twist/writhe test occurred.
- No independent retained coordinate means no memory test occurred.
- A reproduced opposite frozen sign is adverse to the proposed sign mapping and must be carried forward, not relabeled.
