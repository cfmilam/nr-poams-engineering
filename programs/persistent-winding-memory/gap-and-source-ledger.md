## 4. Material gaps before execution

1. **Platform/device family:** no material stack, loop geometry, junction technology, operating temperature or fabrication route selected.
2. **Application thresholds:** hold time, BER, read-disturbance and endurance targets are proposed design goals, not yet tied to a use case and pilot.
3. **Device calibration:** no local state spacing, inductance, mutual coupling, critical current, barrier, read noise, write margin or phase-slip exposure.
4. **B1/B2 realization:** energy paths are specified; angular-momentum reaction sensing/bounds are not instantiated.
5. **Nondestructive read proof:** no measured backaction-versus-read-energy curve.
6. **Controls:** no fabricated open-loop, shorted-turn, electronics-only or witness structures.
7. **State-independent receipt:** no redundant physical readout yet distinguishes loop state from SQUID/electronics latch.
8. **Phase-slip model:** no pilot events/exposure to set censoring, event-merging or temperature/field grid.
9. **Endurance:** no cycle data, drift criteria from apparatus capability or stopping rule.
10. **Native POAMS prediction:** no prospective residual distinct from established superconducting behavior has been derived. Until one exists, the work is register engineering and M0 calibration, not a POAMS-exclusive test.

## 5. Resolved conflicts

- The package does not call coherence, linewidth, ring-down or persistent drive a memory.
- It distinguishes flux from fluxoid and state inference from native raw sensor output.
- It separates nondestructive and destructive readout empirically.
- A zero observed slip count yields a finite upper rate bound, not infinite lifetime.
- Energy closure does not stand in for angular-momentum closure.
- A reproduced extant trapped-flux memory is a qualified platform result, not independent ontological confirmation.
- Superconducting, vortex, magnetic and YIG parameters are not mixed into a fictional representative device.
