## 6. Finite-null and adverse-result meaning

- A qualified open/absent null bounds a named source/external observable over the tested band, window, parasitic census, sensitivity and geometry; it does not establish exact silence.
- Agreement with an imported network model plus direct injections validates the apparatus/accounting and constrains any extra complete-link term. It does not disprove POAMS ontology.
- A detuning null bounds the preregistered readiness dependence in the achieved linewidth/range; it does not test every matching coordinate.
- A whole-apparatus force/torque null bounds net external response, not internal electrode force or strain.
- Internal force with zero external impulse is an ordinary redistribution unless a complete boundary receipt says otherwise.
- Failure of energy closure blocks anomalous-energy language; it is not evidence for new energy.
- A response that follows common-mode, ground, heat, magnetic gradient, switch impulse, cable reaction or software timing is an artifact result worth retaining.
- A reproducible opposite sign/order falsifies the frozen proposed sign/order for that apparatus and may not be relabeled after unblinding.
- No pre-ordinary-response registration means no instantaneous/nonlocal conclusion, regardless of trace appearance.
