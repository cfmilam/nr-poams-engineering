## 4. Conflicts resolved in this package

- The legacy “either sign is positive” posture is rejected. A sign has meaning only against a frozen, physically realized transformation; otherwise the channel is unassigned.
- Static alignment does not by itself prove twist, and driven coning does not prove writhe.
- Persistent phase, long ring-down and a residual balance offset do not prove retention.
- Local electromagnetic force is not the claimed endpoint; the endpoint is a residual whole-apparatus support response after B1/B2 transfer accounting.
- Numerical checks and prior document audits are not physical measurements.
