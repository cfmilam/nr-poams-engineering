# YIG four-account laboratory protocol

## 1. Objective and claim grades

Determine whether preparing one characterized YIG article in distinguishable angular-momentum organizations produces a reproducible change in the **support wrench of the complete apparatus**, after direct measurement or conservative bounding of conventional force, torque, heat, vibration, pressure, electromagnetic and cable-transfer paths.

| Item | Grade before a run |
|---|---|
| YIG ferrimagnetism, FMR craft, magnetometry, microwave resonators | Imported established apparatus physics |
| State coordinates from magnetometry, I/Q, linewidth, ring-down and work receipts | Operational reductions, to be locally calibrated |
| Static order as an intrinsic-twist realization | Prospective POAMS assignment under test |
| Driven coning as physical writhe | Prospective, explicitly **not assumed** |
| Retained post-drive state | Prospective occurrence requiring an independent witness |
| State-linked support response | Prospective empirical relation; no coefficient assumed |
| Any recorded force or state change | M0 only when raw instrument custody and calibration exist |
| Script output, simulation, arithmetic replay | Not physical measurement |

No levitation, propulsion, weight cancellation or technological performance is claimed.

## 2. Falsifiable hypotheses

- **H0-S (static):** after nuisance closure, support wrench is equivalent across registered static directed-order states at the frozen sensitivity.
- **H1-S:** support contrast covaries with the independently measured signed static-state coordinate under the registered physical orientation transformation and survives artifact injections.
- **H0-C (coherent):** at fixed static state, resonant drive produces no support increment beyond matched detuned-RF, absorbed-power, heater and source-reaction controls.
- **H1-C:** at fixed static order, support contrast covaries with measured coherent-state amplitude/trajectory and resonance/ring-down, not generator command or elapsed cycle count.
- **H0-R (retention):** after all registered transients decay, no independently readable signed state remains.
- **H1-R:** an independent signed post-drive coordinate persists, scales with a preregistered intervention, reverses or erases on command, repeats after remount, and closes within the B2 reaction ledger.
- **H0-F (support):** every apparent support response is explained or bounded by measured local magnetic/electrical/cable/thermal/mechanical paths.
- **H1-F:** a residual whole-apparatus support response remains state-covariant after those paths pass their direct-injection rejection tests.

An opposite registered sign is an adverse result, not permission to rename the channel after unblinding.

## 3. Stage gates

### Gate 0 — apparatus qualification

Pass all before physics acquisition:

1. force and six-axis wrench calibration across the full analysis bandwidth, both signs, with nonlinearity and creep bounded;
2. common-clock timing verified by injected synchronous markers;
3. three-axis magnetic-field and gradient maps for every bias polarity, article orientation and coil state;
4. thermal transfer functions from heater injections at article, resonator, cables, mount and sensor electronics;
5. tilt, vibration, acoustic, pressure/gas-flow and cable-force injection maps;
6. RF pickup, ground-loop, amplifier-state, dummy-load, lock-loss and detector-saturation maps;
7. hidden condition labels demonstrated end-to-end;
8. empty mount, mass/geometry-matched dummy and nonresonant article characterized;
9. disassemble/remount reproducibility demonstrated;
10. all B1 and B2 boundary crossings measured or conservatively bounded.

Failure blocks interpretation and triggers redesign; it is not a POAMS null.

### Gate 1 — static directed order

No resonant drive. Randomize reproducible plateaus across both bias senses and at least one perpendicular-orientation family. Record the full vector magnetic path, hysteresis, temperatures, field gradients and complete support wrench. The primary result is a state-pair force contrast or an upper bound. A passed support contrast earns **static directed-order candidate** only; “twist” requires the separate same-transition reaction/work receipt.

### Gate 2 — fixed-alignment drive toggle

Hold the measured static coordinate inside its registered equivalence band. Randomize resonant drive, detuned drive, matched thermal injection, RF-chain dummy, source-reaction injection and sham commands. Sweep only levels and modes frozen in the annex. Establish coherent state from raw I/Q or calibrated vector trajectory, resonance, linewidth, absorbed/returned power and ring-down.

Classify as coherent candidate, coherent null, artifact, or channel-unassigned. Do not multiply bounded state by RF cycle count. A sign may discriminate a frozen operational trajectory only if the reversal really preserves static order, mode, trajectory magnitude, heat, gradient and geometry; otherwise report unsigned covariance.

### Gate 3 — pump-off retention

The analysis window starts only after thresholds for resonator field, magnetic coherence, vibration, RF leakage, electrical settling and thermal derivative are all crossed. Require an independent signed coordinate, dose relation, commanded erase/reverse, support covariance, B2 closure, remount repeat and an out-of-sample article or drive level. A lingering balance offset is drift unless the independent witness passes.

“Retained writhe candidate” additionally requires a measured oriented closed 3-D centerline and framing, frozen Wr/Tw/Lk reductions and reconnection rules, signed reproducible change, associated boundary/link account, and independently calibrated action scale. Oriented solid angle or phase-space loop alone is insufficient.

### Gate 4 — replication

Repeat the frozen package with a second article and independent analyst. Prefer a second apparatus. Analytic replay verifies reduction only; it is not a physical replication.

## 4. Apparatus architecture

Two nested boundaries are mandatory:

- **B1:** article, mount and resonator up to named mechanical, RF, electrical, magnetic, thermal and optical feedthrough planes. It establishes local transfer into the support.
- **B2:** complete reaction apparatus, including bias source/return path, RF source/load/cables, thermal paths and support/Earth connection. It establishes whether an apparent residual is merely exported reaction.

Subsystems:

1. characterized YIG sphere or other simple-demagnetization article on a nonmagnetic, low-creep mount;
2. reversible vector bias and trim system with mapped gradients and monitored source reactions;
3. tunable resonator with forward/reflected I/Q, field map, loaded-Q and ring-down measurement;
4. magnetometry/mode readout independent of the support sensor;
5. primary vertical-force channel plus independent force/strain channel and six-axis wrench measurement about one frozen origin;
6. thermometry/calorimetry, accelerometry, tilt, vibration, acoustics, pressure, humidity, gas-flow and electrostatic monitors;
7. cable-force and fixture-strain monitors, RF enclosure, thermal enclosure and interlocks;
8. common-clock acquisition, command recorder, immutable raw store and hardware event marker.

The primary endpoint is whole-apparatus support response. Local magnetic force, electrode/electrostatic force, Lorentz reaction, cable thrust and Maxwell stress are measured nuisance transfers, not synonyms for that endpoint.

## 5. Specimen characterization

Before randomization record article ID and lot; mass; three orthogonal dimensions; roundness; density consistency; surface finish; crystallographic orientation; mounting axis; remount fiducials; vector magnetization path against field and temperature; hysteresis; saturation criterion; anisotropy; demagnetizing geometry; resonance branches; linewidth; loaded-mode structure; phase; calibrated trajectory or cone coordinate; absorbed/returned power; magnetic and cavity ring-down; thermal diffusivity/expansion response; magnetostriction; and fixture coupling.

Catalogue values size procurement only. Run reductions use local calibrations with uncertainties.

## 6. Exact condition families and controls

The run annex instantiates magnitudes, but the factorial identities are fixed here:

- article: YIG / matched nonmagnetic dummy / nonresonant magnetic control / empty mount;
- bias: positive / negative / zero where stable; plus preregistered perpendicular orientation;
- RF: resonant / detuned with matched delivered and absorbed energy / dummy-load routed / sham command;
- thermal: natural RF heating / matched heater trajectory / no heat;
- coherent sense: paired physical sense states only if matched state equivalence is demonstrated;
- retention: write / sham write / reverse write / erase / no-operation;
- apparatus: normal / sensor swap / cable topology injection / gradient injection / mechanical injection / source-reaction injection;
- reproducibility: initial mount / remount / second article.

Every control is analyzed by the same frozen pipeline. “One reversal rules out artifacts” is prohibited.

## 7. Randomization and blinding

Use balanced permuted blocks stratified by article, orientation and run day. Within each block randomize condition order from a commitment-hashed seed. The scheduler holds minimum washout and thermal-equivalence constraints but does not see force data. Condition codes are escrowed; operators see only safety-required actions. Automated actuation is preferred. Analysts receive opaque codes and remain blind until:

1. all maximum blocks are complete or a registered safety/data-quality stop fires;
2. calibration-before/after checks and frozen exclusions are applied;
3. raw and derived packages are hashed;
4. the primary report is generated and signed.

Emergency unblinds, operator interventions, aborted trials, lock loss, clipping and missing samples remain in custody. No outcome-driven extension is allowed.

## 8. Primary estimands and analysis

For each registered state pair:

\[
\Delta F_{ij}=\bar F_i-\bar F_j.
\]

Estimate it with the preregistered time window and bandwidth using block as the experimental unit. Report confidence/credible intervals, effect and equivalence conclusions, and odd/even components under each reversal. A state-normalized secant may be reported only for a frozen scalar coordinate with nonzero separation; it is local and not a universal law.

The primary model contains condition, block/day, article and the finite list of nuisance covariates validated in Gate 0. The primary conclusion cannot depend on stepwise covariate selection. Robust and unadjusted analyses are sensitivity checks. Time-series tests must include autocorrelation; effective sample size is not raw sample count. Allocate multiplicity separately to static, coherent and retained primary families. Freeze the smallest effect/equivalence region from achieved calibration and engineering relevance before physics data.

Required diagnostics: state covariance; resonance specificity; latency; decay relative to cavity/magnetic/mechanical/thermal ring-down; dose curve; reversal parity; source-reaction closure; dummy/empty response; remount and article heterogeneity; residual spectrum; influence of exclusions; and predictive performance of the nuisance model on artifact-injection data.

## 9. Nuisance and error budget

The annex assigns measured 1-sigma uncertainty, conservative bound, correlation and calibration ID to each:

- force zero, scale, nonlinearity, creep, hysteresis and off-axis sensitivity;
- magnetic gradients acting on article, mount, resonator and sensor;
- magnetostriction and fixture strain;
- RF radiation pressure/Maxwell stress, pickup, rectification, ground current and cable reaction;
- bias-coil mechanical reaction and current-dependent heating;
- resonator pressure and dielectric/electrostrictive motion;
- thermal expansion, convection, buoyancy, radiometry, thermocouple wire force and sensor self-heating;
- vibration, acoustic coupling, tilt, building motion and support relaxation;
- pressure, humidity, sorption and electrostatic charge;
- digitizer noise, clock skew, filtering, aliasing, clipping, dropped samples and servo-state errors;
- operator, order, carryover, remount, day and specimen effects.

Quadrature combination is allowed only for justified independent terms. Correlated terms use covariance or conservative coherent addition. Any unmeasured boundary crossing blocks a residual claim.

## 10. Preregistered decision rules

- **State not achieved:** no physical test of that branch occurred.
- **Qualified state + equivalence to zero:** scoped null at stated sensitivity.
- **Force without independent state covariance:** apparatus response.
- **Response reproduced by a direct artifact injection or dummy:** artifact-bound.
- **Static covariance only:** static directed-order candidate; no coherent/retained inference.
- **Drive covariance that follows measured coherence and dies with ring-down:** bounded coherent candidate, channel unassigned unless its separate receipt passes.
- **Post-drive independent state without B2 closure:** unexplained occurrence requiring a wider ledger, not retention/writhe.
- **Independent state + registered transform + erase/reverse + closure + physical repeat:** retained-state candidate eligible for replication.
- **Opposite frozen sign/order/axis after artifact gates:** repeat once; if reproduced with M0 custody, retire the contradicted mapping at that scope.

Significance alone never promotes a result. All positive branches require the corresponding equivalence, artifact, closure and repeat gates.

## 11. Useful null consequences

- Static null bounds support coupling to the achieved directed-order coordinate for this article, geometry and bandwidth; it does not null all intrinsic spin hypotheses.
- Coherent null bounds incremental support coupling to the achieved mode/trajectory and power regime; it ends cycle-accumulation claims for that regime.
- Retention null says the tested YIG architecture is a coherent bus, not a retained register; it redirects memory work to engineered pinning, defects, coupled accepting layers or other materials.
- B2-closed support null rejects the tested state-to-support transfer while preserving YIG as a calibration platform for angular-momentum transfer.
- Artifact-positive results improve force-metrology and apparatus design even when the POAMS branch fails.
- Failure to match physical sense states means the proposed signed twist/writhe discriminator was not performed; the remedy is state engineering, not reinterpretation.

## 12. Safety

RF-open interlock; leakage survey for every state; magnet exclusion zone and projectile control; current, stored-energy and thermal limits; amplifier reflected-power/arc trips; guarded pinch zones; article containment; remote ramp from minimum power; emergency stop; and logged abort thresholds. Safety overrides blinding, with access recorded.

## 13. Downstream dependencies

Results feed, but do not automatically validate: the twist/writhe partition law; mechanical-gyro comparison; navigation/Variable-G support claims; persistent winding memory and gate mathematics; Einstein–de Haas/Barnett accounting; Polyverse parity; and any force-actuator concept. The retained branch depends on a presently missing independent readout architecture. The support interpretation depends on full B1/B2 closure, not merely better balance sensitivity.

## 14. Tariff reciprocity acceptance gate (amendment 2026-09-27)

**Operational calibration (Coulomb-free).** Over a declared closed measurement window, with reservoir postings bounded by the §9 budget, define the magnetic angular-momentum change from the closed mechanical receipt:

```
ΔJ_mag ≡ −ΔL_body   (closed window)
γ_eff  ≡ V·ΔM / ΔJ_mag
```

from co-registered magnetometry and mechanical-impulse records. This defines the specimen tariff from receipts. It imports no e/2m value and no electron-substance ontology.

**Gate R — reciprocity.** On the same specimen in the same preparation state:

1. Measure γ_EdH: drive magnetization; read the closed mechanical angular impulse.
2. Measure γ_Barnett: drive rotation; read the magnetization change against the declared small-signal comparator (§5), respecting its hysteresis/anisotropy/domain validity limits.
3. The gate passes iff the two tariffs agree within the preregistered combined uncertainty.

Under one-substance bookkeeping this equality is the structural expectation; empirically it is a test, not a given. A failure blocks all downstream sign interpretation (Gates 2–4) for that specimen state and is reported as an adverse calibration result — never repaired by post-hoc reweighting.

**Cross-reference.** Magnetometry and angular momentum are distinct accounts: an M-null never certifies a J-null (data schema §9).
