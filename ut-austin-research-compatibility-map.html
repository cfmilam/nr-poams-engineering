Executive judgment

UT’s advantage is a short path from apparatus to independent characterization.

UT Austin can materially accelerate magnetic-state, resonant mechanical, nanofabrication, optical, and material-state work. Its most useful front door is not a broad theory campaign or a single institutional champion. It is a bounded portfolio routed through the Microelectronics Research Center, Texas Nanofabrication Facility, Texas Materials Institute, and named experimental groups.

The strongest first nucleus: Jean Anne Incorvia for spintronics, nanomagnetism, and MRC technical leadership; Roberto Panepucci for facility feasibility; Ruochen Lu for resonant acoustic and MEMS controls; Elaine Li for ultrafast material-state measurements; and an independent statistician.

The research contract

Five gates for every engagement

The invitation is an instrument-and-null problem. It is never “validate POAMS.”

1 · Native intervention

Name the circulation, winding, phase, twist/writhe partition, or closure condition being changed.

2 · Raw observable

Name the voltage, displacement, torque, support record, phase, spectrum, image, or count before interpretation.

3 · Frozen discriminator

Fix the sign, ordering, reversal, threshold, scaling law, or null before the run.

4 · Direct controls

Measure thermal, magnetic, electrical, acoustic, mechanical, timing, and analyst transfer paths.

5 · Technological leverage

State what reliable control of the measured occurrence would permit us to build.

Standing exclusions

  • No department-wide ontology pitch.
  • No interpretation before raw occurrence.
  • No advanced apparatus before lower-stage gates pass.

Priority 1 · material-state metrology

Elaine Li

Ultrafast spectroscopy · spintronics · coherent optical control · TQI leadership

Strong fit for witnessing reversible material states and phase dynamics before any rate-of-time interpretation is attached.

First ask: define the least interpretive optical record that proves the material state changed.

Priority 2 · quantum devices

Emanuel Tutuc

Quantum-confined systems · novel semiconductors · transport devices

Useful after a candidate state, geometry, or transport signature is fixed tightly enough to justify a custom nanoscale device.

Stop rule: no bespoke device while the lower-complexity occurrence remains ambiguous.

Priority 2 · analysis firewall

James G. Scott—or equivalent

Multiple testing · high-dimensional inference · independent analysis

A candidate independent statistician for multiplicity, model flexibility, stopping rules, and held-out analysis.

Structural rule: analysis authority remains independent of the effect-seeking laboratory.

Experiment teams

Match people to actual debts

Each campaign begins with an apparatus decision or raw observable, not an interpretation.

A

Magnetic alignment and driven phase response

Team: Incorvia · MRC staff · Lu for acoustic injection · independent statistician.

First objective: separate static alignment from driven phase advance while measuring thermal, magnetic-gradient, acoustic, cable-force, and support transfer.

B

Persistent winding memory

Team: Incorvia · Tutuc · MRC/TMI · independent readout critic.

First objective: demonstrate a retained, reversible, addressable state with a frozen transition rule and an independently readable record.

C

Mechanically ordered angular momentum

Team: RMC build · Lu/Hall controls · independent statistician · outside replication site.

First objective: close resonant, acoustic, piezoelectric, magnetic, thermal, aerodynamic, and support paths before any anomalous inertial reading.

D

Rate-of-time material state

Team: Elaine Li · Incorvia or Tutuc · TMI · QLab only when operationally confirmed.

First objective: establish a reversible material-state signature in raw optical/electrical records before attaching a rate interpretation.

E

Receiver and three-route optical work

Team: Burghoff, Wasserman, Fan, or MRC photonics selected by the exact wavelength, bandwidth, state, and detector debt.

Sequence: no advanced receiver build until its lower-stage source, loss, noise, and pooled-null gates physically pass.

Infrastructure

Facilities that change the timetable

External access and expert staff make UT unusually practical for RMC’s Austin-based work.

MRC / TNF

A 12,000-square-foot shared cleanroom with lithography, deposition, etch, microscopy, metrology, and electrical evaluation. External users may enter through a project proposal, service agreement, background check, UT onboarding, safety training, and equipment training. Published policy says facility users retain their IP.

Texas Materials Institute

More than 65 major instruments and over $40 million in processing and characterization infrastructure, with external users, industry participation, and Ph.D.-level facility managers. Best for materials screening and independent surface, structural, electrical, optical, and microscopic characterization.

Texas Quantum Institute / QLab

TQI organizes quantum materials, metrology, and systems across campus. QLab is being established as a quantum-enhanced semiconductor metrology facility for academic and industry users. Treat it as an emerging option until instruments, access rules, and schedules are confirmed.

Institutional architecture

Keep three engagement rails separate

Facility access, university research, and personal advice carry different authority and obligations.

1 · External facility use

RMC submits a project to MRC or contacts the relevant TMI manager, executes the required service arrangement, trains named users, and buys instrument time or assisted service. This is the fastest route to apparatus facts.

2 · Sponsored research

Use UT’s Office of Sponsored Projects for defined university research with a named PI, deliverables, publication and IP terms, budget, milestones, and stop gates. Individual faculty cannot bind the university.

3 · Personal consulting

Use for bounded design criticism and strategy. UT states that consulting is a private agreement, requires prior approval, and must remain consistent with university duties. It does not provide university staff, laboratories, or institutional endorsement.

Execution

A disciplined first 90 days

Exploit proximity, but keep RMC apparatus, UT facilities, and sponsored work visibly distinct.

Days 1–30

Resolve feasibility

  • One process brief to MRC.
  • One characterization brief to TMI.
  • One frozen magnetic or mechanical discriminator.
  • Facility access, training, and cost map.
  • Independent analysis requirements.
Days 30–90

Freeze one campaign

  • Short PI design review.
  • Declare raw channels and artifact injections.
  • Build an inert or non-effect-seeking apparatus rehearsal.
  • Execute facility agreement or one bounded SRA.
  • Lock custody and stopping rules.
After first result

Let the receipt decide

  • Null: preserve it and close the exact premise.
  • Positive: repeat with independent operators and hardware.
  • Add QLab or advanced photonics only for a remaining debt.
  • Prototype only after independent repetition.
The first ask to UT

“We are not asking UT Austin to endorse POAMS. We want to determine whether one tightly bounded apparatus can be fabricated and tested with its intervention, raw observables, artifact injections, null conditions, stopping rules, and data custody fixed before the run. We need a process-feasibility judgment, the right experimental critic, and the correct institutional route.”
