Before the Pencil

Begin where Plato and Aristotle began: not with a formula, but with the deepest questions about being, knowledge, and explanation. Look inward before drawing outward. A pencil can unfold a premise with extraordinary precision; it cannot make an incoherent premise real.

Right can reinforce right, and wrong can reinforce wrong. The direction of the first step matters because every later success feeds back into the assumptions that produced it.

How error seals itself

  1. A formalism predicts a limited class of observations.
  2. Its symbols are promoted from useful terms into literal entities.
  3. Those entities generate a worldview that is mistaken for nature.
  4. The worldview decides which questions, careers, and instruments are legitimate.
  5. New observations are interpreted inside the same worldview until the model decides what counts as a test of itself.

How inquiry corrects itself

  1. Natural philosophy: ask what can coherently exist and what would count as an explanation.
  2. POAMS: state the native physical organization—circulation, closure, exchange, twist, writhe, or natural orbit.
  3. Mathematics: represent that bookkeeping without allowing notation to invent the ontology.
  4. Experiment: name the raw observable and failure condition, leaving nature the power of veto.
  5. Engineering: use what survives, and return what the instrument teaches to the beginning of the inquiry.

Normal Realism came first; POAMS followed from it. Mathematics serves the physics, experiment tests it, and engineering earns the consequence. The loop remains healthy only while nature can still say no.
