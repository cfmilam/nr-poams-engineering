Continuous Nonlocal Refiling

A two-seat trapped-ion protocol for asking one operational question: does a continuously controlled joint process contain a reversible generator that cannot be reconstructed from independently calibrated local controls?

Experiment ready for physical implementation · frozen design and analysis

The decisive test

Intervention: reverse the signed area or duration of a bichromatic Mølmer–Sørensen control while holding independently calibrated local programs fixed.

Raw observable: both ions’ unthresholded state-dependent-fluorescence photon counts, with exposure, pulse, image/channel and apparatus telemetry.

Primary claim: the odd derivative of the reconstructed joint operational map lies outside the frozen span of every independently calibrated lifted local generator.

No density matrix, Hamiltonian form, Born-rule fit or entanglement fidelity enters the primary discriminator. Those are permitted only as labeled comparisons after the operational output is frozen.

Named apparatus

Two co-trapped \(^{171}\mathrm{Yb}^{+}\) ions in a linear Paul trap provide the independently addressable seats. Imported engineering uses the ground-manifold clock states conventionally labeled \(|0\rangle=|{}^2S_{1/2},F=0,m_F=0\rangle\) and \(|1\rangle=|{}^2S_{1/2},F=1,m_F=0\rangle\), individual Raman controls, transverse collective motion for the joint control, and per-ion fluorescence collection.

Hardware precedent: Zhang et al. (2020). Dedicated two-ion MS process-tomography precedent: Harty et al. (2021). Together these precedents place the required two-ion apparatus and process reconstruction within demonstrated laboratory reach.

The scale \(\epsilon\) is fixed by an independent engineering calibration before primary acquisition. Primary outcomes may not choose it. Run order is recorded before execution; analysis receives hashed sign/sham labels until reconstruction is frozen.

Primary analysis

Build operational probability tables from fluorescence records. Establish local preparation/effect ranks on training blocks and test held-out prediction. Whiten local preparation/effect Gram matrices using training blocks only; freeze those coordinate transforms. Lift the independently measured local derivatives into the product-coordinate space and freeze their span \(\mathcal L\).

Estimate the odd joint derivatives

G₁ = [T(+ε) − T(−ε)] / (2ε) — primary

G₂ = [T(+2ε) − T(−2ε)] / (4ε) — curvature and step-size challenge

The primary metric is held-out multinomial log-likelihood improvement of an unrestricted odd derivative over the derivative constrained to \(\mathcal L\). Use a stratified temporal-block bootstrap: 10,000 replicates, seed 20261001, simultaneous 99% coverage, and no missing-block imputation.

Pass: the simultaneous lower bound exceeds zero and the simultaneous upper envelope from every mandatory sham/cross-talk family, while predicting held-out cells and retaining its classification under lawful independent local fiducial reparameterizations.

Kill gates

  1. Local operational rank is unstable across held-out blocks.
  2. Unregistered loss, leakage or a third outcome exceeds its frozen bound.
  3. A held-out joint effect defeats local tomography.
  4. \(G_1\) and \(G_2\) disagree beyond the frozen curvature model.
  5. The reversal/echo fails its frozen tolerance.
  6. The nonlocal residual remains inside the sham/cross-talk envelope.
  7. Held-out product-setting prediction fails.
  8. Lawful local fiducial reparameterization changes the local/nonlocal classification.

No gate may be repaired after unblinding by dropping shots, changing \(\epsilon\), adding effects or changing rank. A revision begins a new preregistration.

Raw custody

Every shot retains run/block/shot IDs, scheduled and executed pulse hashes, blinded control label, both photon counts and detector channels, exposure/controller timestamps, ion-presence and image flags, cooling/pumping/analysis receipts, Raman and trap telemetry, field monitor, gain/background/cross-talk calibrations, retries, aborts and missing records. Raw bundle, schedule, controller source, analysis source and environment lock are hashed before labels open.

What success opens

A successful independent replay would establish a genuinely joint reversible generator beyond the calibrated local repertoire—an immediately useful primitive for reversible logic, correlation synthesis and transduction. The frozen gates determine exactly how far that result reaches.
