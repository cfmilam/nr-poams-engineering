# Mechanically Ordered Angular Momentum

## Program question

Does a guarded mechanical rotor produce a reproducible whole-apparatus response odd under a physically realized reversal of rotor angular momentum relative to the containing angular-momentum organization, after bearing, drive, support, cable, magnetic, aerodynamic, thermal, vibration, tilt, and orientation paths are measured?

The design keeps steady bulk spin, acceleration transients, support reaction, Earth orientation, twist/writhe classification, and retained state separate.
