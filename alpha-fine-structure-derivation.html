1. The one formula

Let $t$ be an atom's intrinsic (proper) spectral period, $t_R$ its period as registered by an observer, and $s$ their separation. Since $s/c$ is a duration, the three measures compose orthogonally, and Pythagoras' theorem gives (Pope, App. 1):

$$t_R^2 = \left(\tfrac{s}{c}\right)^2 + t^2.$$

Setting $s=v\,t_R$ and simplifying returns the time-dilation relation — from geometry alone, with $c$ a units-conversion constant between length and time, not a velocity:

$$t_R=\frac{t}{\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}}.$$

This is the whole of the physics that follows. Drag the tilt below: the observer's registered time $t_R$ is the hypotenuse; as the motion-measure $s/c$ grows, $t_R$ stretches. There is no "moving light" here — only the geometry of proper and relative time.

2. First level: the spectrum, from discrete time

The one physical premise of Normal Realism: time is a discretum. So $t_R$ and $t$ take only discrete values, and their ratio is rational, $t_R/t=n/N$ for integers $n,N$ (Pope, App. 3). Substituting into the time formula and using $e=mv^2$ gives, by algebra alone,

$$\frac{n^2}{N^2}=\frac{1}{1-v^2/c^2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad e=mc^2\!\left(1-\frac{N^2}{n^2}\right).$$

Dividing by Planck's constant $h$ turns energy into frequency, with $mc^2/h\equiv cR$ the Rydberg limit — and this is the Balmer–Rydberg formula:

$$f = N^2 cR\!\left(\frac{1}{n_1^2}-\frac{1}{n_2^2}\right).$$
DERIVED Balmer and Rydberg fitted this; Bohr explained it with electrodynamics POAMS discards; POAMS derives it from time dilation + discreteness. Matching the measured lines fixes $m=2.425473\times10^{-35}$ kg — the mass-equivalent of the light-quantum (the "photum"), the most an atom can shed or absorb per interaction.

3. Second level: gravitation is orbital time dilation

The atom's constituents carry angular momentum. For circular motion of $m$ about $M$, conservation of angular momentum alone gives the natural-orbit condition — no in vacuo force invoked (Osborne; Pope, App. 4):

$$v^2=\frac{GM}{r}.$$

Substituting into the same time formula yields gravitational time dilation directly:

$$t_R=\frac{t}{\sqrt{1-GM/(c^2 r)}}.$$
DERIVED This is the clock-rate law confirmed by GPS and in strong gravity — the same single substitution that produced the spectrum. In Normal Realism "gravitation" is this orbital time dilation; there is no separate gravitational force. Bring the clock closer to the mass below and watch it slow.

4. Fine structure is orbital time dilation

Here the two levels meet. An atom (Pope, App. 5) is a configuration of orbital plus spin angular momentum — balanced masses circulating a barycentre, with no "charge" (the conventional electrostatic charge is re-expressed as spin kinetic energy in joules). That internal circulation has an orbital velocity fixed by $L=\hbar$:

$$v_{\text{orb}}=2.1876907\times10^{6}\ \text{m s}^{-1},\qquad r=\hbar/mv_{\text{orb}}=5.29\times10^{-11}\ \text{m},$$

i.e. exactly the Bohr speed and radius. Its ratio to $c$ is the fine-structure constant:

$$\frac{v_{\text{orb}}}{c}=7.297\times10^{-3}=\alpha.$$

Now apply the time formula a second time, to this internal circulation. Its own time dilation is

$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-v_{\text{orb}}^2/c^2}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-\alpha^2}}\approx 1+\tfrac12\alpha^2.$$

Fine structure is not a new phenomenon: it is orbital time dilation superimposed on the gross spectrum — the second level of the one formula. The pattern comes from the discreteness of the orbital motion; the scale from $\alpha^2=(v_{\text{orb}}/c)^2$. This is the same relativistic origin standard physics assigns to fine structure, reached from the single Pythagorean equation rather than the Dirac equation.

5. What $\alpha$ is — and what it is not

The textbook definition $\alpha=e^2/4\pi\varepsilon_0\hbar c$ bundles three shadows: electric charge, the permittivity of empty space, and $c$-as-a-speed. Read through POAMS, the same measured number is simply

$$\alpha=\frac{v_{\text{orb}}}{c}=\text{the atom's internal orbital speed, in units of }c.$$
SCOPE POAMS derives the role of $\alpha$ in full: it is the orbital-velocity ratio whose square, entering through a second application of the time formula, sets the fine-structure scale. POAMS does not derive the numerical value of that orbital velocity from geometry — and neither does the Dirac equation, QED, or the Standard Model, all of which take $\alpha$ as measured input. What is distinctive here is that the value is given a plain physical identity — an orbital speed — rather than left an unexplained coupling, and that a single premise ties it to spectrum, mass–energy, and gravity alike.
